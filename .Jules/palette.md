## 2025-05-18 - WCAG Color Contrast & Tooltips in Guard HomeScreen Status Badges
**Learning:** Light text (white) on bright warning colors like yellow/orange fails contrast ratios and hinders readability for security personnel. Using contrasting text colors (dark text on light warning backgrounds, or distinct accessible badge colors) significantly improves visual clarity.
**Action:** Always check chip/badge background vs text foreground color contrast, and provide clear `tooltip` attributes on icon actions.
